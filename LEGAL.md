## 4. Asset provenance

- **No ripped or copied assets.** The repository contains **no** images, textures, 3D models, audio, music, video, fonts or logos taken from the films, from official games or from any other third-party source.
- **Everything is generated by code at runtime.** Every model, texture, shader, sound effect and music track is produced procedurally by the Game's original source code. Ship and location designs are original low-level geometric reinterpretations built from code, not reproductions of official models or blueprints.
- **Original music.** The soundtrack is an original, procedurally generated score. It does **not** use, quote or imitate the compositions of John Williams or any other film music.
- **No official logos or fonts.** The *Star Wars* logo and official typefaces are not used. Titles are rendered with generic system fonts. Text written in the style of an opening crawl is original text.
- **Voices.** Spoken lines, when enabled, are produced by the player's own browser text-to-speech engine. No recordings of actors' voices are included or used.
- **Dialogue.** Radio subtitles are mostly original writing. A limited number of **short lines of dialogue are quoted or paraphrased** from *Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope* (1977, written and directed by George Lucas, © Lucasfilm Ltd.), in English and in Italian rendering.
